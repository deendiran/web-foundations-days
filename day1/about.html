About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web application designed to help you capture your ideas and notes quickly. Whether you're brainstorming, jotting down reminders, or keeping track of tasks, QuickNotes provides a straightforward interface to manage your notes efficiently.

How to Use QuickNotes

  1. Fill out the form on the home page with a title and content.
  2. Click "Add Note" to save your note.
  3. View all your notes in the list below.

Features

  • Add and save notes with titles and content.
  • View a list of all your saved notes.
  • Simple and user-friendly interface.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Here are some useful keyboard shortcuts:

Shortcut Action
